FAQ
Are vendor workflows available on all ApprovalMax plans?
Vendor workflows are available on Advanced and Premium plans.
Can I restrict who can create vendors in ApprovalMax?
Yes. You define specific requesters and approvers, giving you strict segregation of duties: the people who propose suppliers are never the people who approve them.
Do vendor workflows sync with my accounting software?
Yes. Once approved, vendor details are pushed automatically to Xero, QuickBooks Online, or Oracle NetSuite. In Xero, this type of workflow is known as a contact workflow.
Can I use vendor workflows to update existing supplier details?
No. Vendor workflows are currently for vetting and adding new suppliers only.
What details can approvers check before a supplier is approved?
Reviewers see the full proposed vendor record during approval, including supplier details, bank details, tax codes, and GL categories, and can query anything before it reaches your books.
Do approvers need access to my accounting system?
No. Everyone involved in vetting a new supplier works in ApprovalMax, by email, or in the mobile app. Nobody needs a Xero, QuickBooks Online, or NetSuite login just to sign off.
Can I set different approval paths for different types of supplier?
Yes. Route low-risk suppliers through a shorter approval and high-risk or high-value ones through additional steps, so vetting depth matches the supplier.
