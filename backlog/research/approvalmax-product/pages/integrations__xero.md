The approval layer your Xero business is missing

ApprovalMax sits between Xero and every outgoing payment - automatically routing invoices and purchase orders through the right approval chains, enforcing your spending policies, and creating a permanent audit record as a by-product of the process.

Your AP lifecycle

Control every stage - from the moment spend is committed to the moment payment leaves the bank.

Capture

Invoices and expenses enter Xero accurately and automatically - so you start at review, not at cleanup. Spend committed outside the system is surfaced the moment it enters, before it becomes a problem on the ledger.

Approve

The most sophisticated approval logic in the market - routing every invoice to the right person, with the right budget context, enforced by hardcoded policy. Every approval decision is permanently recorded.

Detect risk

Duplicate invoices, suspicious bank detail changes, budget overruns - caught at the approval stage, before money leaves the business. Detection gets smarter over time.

Fraud and audit

Most approval tools stop the moment an invoice is signed off. ApprovalMax closes that gap - extending governance all the way to payment, without touching existing Xero workflows or banking setups.

Why ApprovalMax + Xero
The governance layer Xero doesn't have

Xero is your accounting system. ApprovalMax is the layer that controls what reaches it. Every invoice, purchase order, and expense passes through the right approval chain before it touches Xero — automatically, with a full audit record attached.



Your approvers don't need to be in Xero

Give approvers access to exactly what they need to make a decision - without giving them access to your accounting system. Protect your Xero data while keeping the approval process moving.

 





Xero-native features that go further

Xero Purchase Matching (XPM), live budget syncing from Xero, and support for Classes and Tracking Categories - ApprovalMax uses what's already in your Xero to enforce the controls your business needs.

 





Built for your existing tech stack

ApprovalMax works alongside the tools you already use for data capture and payment. It syncs directly with Xero - no rekeying, no duplicate entry, no disruption to workflows you've already built.

 





How it works
ApprovalMax connects directly with Xero - syncing bills, purchase orders, and expenses into approval workflows in real time. Set up once, and every transaction that matters routes to the right person automatically.

Built for every stage of your Accounts Payable journey
Approval automation

Set up approval workflows that follow your delegation of authority - and let them run. Invoices route to the right person automatically, substitution rules prevent stalls, and every decision is permanently recorded.





Bill processing

Bills sync automatically from Xero into ApprovalMax, move through your approval workflow, and push back to Xero once approved. Nothing gets paid without the right sign-off - and no one has to rekey a thing.





Purchase order management

Create, approve, and send purchase orders to suppliers - all in one place. Spend is committed with the right authority before work starts, not after.





Bill-to-PO matching

Match incoming bills against already-approved purchase orders before processing payment. Catch discrepancies before they become payment errors - not during reconciliation.





Budget checking

See live budget position at the moment of approval - so overspends are caught before they happen, not discovered after the invoice is paid.
