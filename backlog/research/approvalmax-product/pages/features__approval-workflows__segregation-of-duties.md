FAQ
Do I need a specific plan to set up segregation of duties in ApprovalMax?
No. The settings that create segregation of duties, approval conditions and user roles, are part of every plan. There's nothing to add on.
How many approvers can I add to the system?
The number of approvers depends on the plan size you choose: Small, Medium, and Large, with Large size offering unlimited approvers. Find out more in our Pricing plan.
Do approvers need access to my accounting system?
No. Approvers work entirely inside ApprovalMax. Your live books stay restricted to your Finance team.
Can I set up different rules for approvers and requesters during trial?
Yes. The 14-day free trial gives you the full product, so you can build and test your separation rules before you decide.
Can auditors see the record themselves?
Yes. The Auditor role is view-only across workflows, requests and the approval matrix, and can generate reports. Nobody has to hand over an approver login to give an auditor visibility.
Can someone approve a request they raised themselves?
Only if your rules allow it. Requester is a condition in the approval matrix, so you can set a step to route past whoever raised the request.
