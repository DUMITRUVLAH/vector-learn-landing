FAQ
Which plans include the mobile app and Slack integration?
Slack alerts, email notifications and the mobile app are available to all users on any plan (Standard, Advanced and Premium).
Can we use the mobile app during the trial?
Yes. The 14-day trial is the full product, so your approvers can test email, mobile and Slack the same way they would use them day-to-day.
Do approvers have to open your accounting system to approve something?
They can act on email, mobile app, web app or Slack. Nothing about the decision requires them to access your ledger.
What can approvers do in Slack?
Slack notifies them when their approval is needed, and they can approve, reject or comment from there.
Does a decision made by email or Slack get recorded the same way?
Yes. No matter where the decision is made, it lands in the same approval history: the approver, the action, the timestamp and any comments. Nudges are logged too.
Can I scan documents with ApprovalMax Capture in the mobile app?
Yes, currently only available for Xero users uploading bills.
Can approvers choose which notifications they get?
Yes, users can customise notification preferences for email in their profile settings.  For Slack, manage organisation-specific alerts by going to the Home tab in Slack and using the settings toggle
Does the mobile app work on tablets?
no, the ApprovalMax app works on mobile devices.
What happens when an approver is on holiday?
An approver can assign a substitute, and requests are routed to them while the approver is away. Nothing waits in a queue for someone to come back.
