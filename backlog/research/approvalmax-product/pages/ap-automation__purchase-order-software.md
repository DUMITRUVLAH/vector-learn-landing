FAQ
How do I choose the best software for purchase order approval?
Since purchase orders are often created by non-accounting staff, consider how easy it is to give access to as many people as you need and whether it's possible to set up rules and restrictions for the purchase order requesters. As for the approval part, you need to make sure that the system is flexible enough to recreate the workflow you need. With ApprovalMax the number of users is unlimited, just as the possibilities of setting up a complex workflow.
Which accounting systems does ApprovalMax Purchase Order software work with?
ApprovalMax connects to Xero, QuickBooks Online, and Oracle NetSuite. Purchase orders can be created in ApprovalMax or pulled in automatically from your accounting system, and approved POs are pushed back ready for processing.
Which plan is Purchase Order approval workflow available on?
Purchase Order approval workflow is available on all plans: Standard, Advanced, and Premium
Can I test Purchase Order approval software during the trial?
Yes. Purchase Order workflow is included in your 14-day free trial; no credit card needed.
Can approvers sign off purchase orders on mobile?
Yes. Approvers can review and approve purchase orders from the ApprovalMax mobile app for iOS and Android, by email, or through Slack notifications.
Can I create a bill from an approved purchase order?
Yes. You can copy an approved purchase order to create a new bill, which saves re-keying the details.
