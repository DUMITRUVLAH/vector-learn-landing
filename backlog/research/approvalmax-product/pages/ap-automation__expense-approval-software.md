FAQ
Who needs expense approval software?

Businesses of all sizes benefit from strengthening their financial controls, improving expense tracking, and staying on top of budget policies with expense approval software. It's especially useful for organisations with complex approval matrices and those looking to reduce the risk of fraud and errors.

How does expense approval software help with reporting?

Expense approval software improves expense reporting by automating the approval process, making sure all expenses are properly reviewed before being recorded in the general ledger. This improves accuracy, provides real-time insights, and makes it easy to comply with company policies.

Can I create and approve expense requests in the mobile app?
Yes, expenses can be created in the mobile app, and approvers can review and sign off from the app, by email, or on the web.
How does the expense creation and approval workflow for QuickBooks Online work?

Expenses can be created directly in ApprovalMax or pulled from Dext. They then go through your approval workflow, and when all approvals are in place, the approved expense and its automatically generated audit report are pushed to QuickBooks Online.

Can I pull expenses from QuickBooks Online into ApprovalMax?
Yes. ApprovalMax supports pulling existing expenses from QuickBooks Online, but these expenses will not run through the approval process in ApprovalMax.
