Why ApprovalMax + NetSuite?
Get up and running in no time

With ApprovalMax, it only takes a few days to connect, set up, and onboard your team – even for complex accounting workflows.

Reduce your software subscription costs

With ApprovalMax, there’s no need to purchase extra licences or employ consultants to build extra modules.

Seamless adoption and happy approvers

Approvers are automatically notified when they need to review a document and can easily approve via web, email, and Slack channels in just a few clicks.

Faster approvals

25% of bills are approved within 2 hours and 50% within one day using ApprovalMax. Take the hassle out of your workflows to secure sign-off quicker.

Workflows and benefits
Approval automation

Set up approval workflows that follow your delegation of authority.

Bill processing

Purchase order management

Easily create, approve, then send POs to suppliers.

Audit trail

Track all approvals with detailed audit trails that can easily be exported and sent to local tax authorities by auditors.

Custom reports

Fraud detection

Stand-alone workflows

Approve non-accounting requests, like time off, travel, or legal contracts.
