How ApprovalMax records and checks every decision

 

Every approval, reported and locked

ApprovalMax builds a report for every document showing exactly who approved it and when, alongside the document details and line items.

Auditors collect what they need

A dedicated read-only Auditor role means you hand over a login instead of compiling evidence on their behalf. They see how your approval workflows actually run, without any risk of changing anything.

See what skipped the workflow

When a document is approved or changed directly in your accounting system, it gets a bypass label and your admins are alerted. The gaps in the trail are visible, so you know what needs explaining before an auditor asks.

Duplicate bills get caught before approval

Every bill is checked against what's already in ApprovalMax, including those that have already been approved. The warning reaches whoever is reviewing it, before the bill goes any further.

Integrate with some of the world’s best platforms

Connect ApprovalMax to Xero, QuickBooks Online, or NetSuite with audit reports attached, so the record travels with the decision.

FAQ
Which plans include audit reports and fraud detection?
All of them. Automatic audit reports, scheduled reports, skipped-approval alerts and the Auditor role are on all plans: Standard, Advanced, and Premium.
Is there a limit on how many audit reports I can generate?
No. An audit report is generated automatically for each document that reaches final approval, and reporting is unlimited on every plan.
Do approvers need access to my accounting system?
No. Approvers work via our web app, mobile app or email, without a licence for your accounting platform.
Does adding my auditor cost extra?
No. Bringing an auditor in for audit season does not change your bill.
Can I try to generate an audit report during the trial?
Yes. Everything on this page is available during your free trial.
Is ApprovalMax secure enough for our financial data?
Yes. ApprovalMax is ISO 27001 certified and built for handling financial documents and approval records. Full details of our certifications, data handling, and security practices are in our trust centre. https://security.approvalmax.com
