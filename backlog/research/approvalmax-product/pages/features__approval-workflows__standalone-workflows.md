FAQ
What are standalone workflows, and how do they differ from standard approvals in ApprovalMax?
Standalone workflows, sometimes called custom approval workflows, operate independently of your accounting software. While standard workflows sync with Xero, QuickBooks Online, or NetSuite, standalone workflows let you run approvals for anything using the same approval logic, from legal contracts to HR leave requests.
Do I need to connect my accounting software to use them?
No. Standalone workflows run entirely within your ApprovalMax Organisation without any external integration, so you can centralise all business approvals in one secure place.
What kinds of requests can I run through a standalone workflow?
Standalone workflows approve any process with a decision requirement: documents like contracts and legal agreements, and requests such as time off, capex requests, IT access and supplier onboarding. You define the request form and the approval path.

Which plan are standalone workflows available on?
Standalone workflows are available on the Premium plan. Every plan includes a free trial, so you can try them before upgrading.
How many standalone workflows can I create?
 As many as you need. There's no limit on standalone workflow creation.
Can I customise the data captured in these workflows?
Yes. Create custom fields such as a project code, department ID, or priority level, so the request form matches your exact internal requirements.
Is there an audit trail for standalone workflows?
Yes. Every completed request generates a comprehensive, timestamped PDF audit report, so every internal decision is documented without anyone scrambling for evidence.
