FAQ
What is Capture?
It's an optical character recognition (OCR) tool, living natively in ApprovalMax. Capture reads uploaded documents and extracts important fields, such as supplier, invoice number, amounts and tax.
Which plans include ApprovalMax Capture?

ApprovalMax Capture is available in all plans, including Standard, Advanced, and Premium.

Which accounting platforms does ApprovalMax Capture work with?
Capture is available to businesses, accountants and bookkeepers using Xero, QuickBooks Online, and NetSuite
Can I try ApprovalMax Capture in the free trial?
Yes. ApprovalMax Capture is included in your 14-day free trial; no credit card needed.
What documents can ApprovalMax Capture process?

It depends on your accounting platform:

1) Xero: bills, purchase orders, credit notes and sales invoices

2) QuickBooks Online: bills and expenses

3) NetSuite: bills

What languages does Capture work with?
ApprovalMax Capture can recognise and import bills in more than 40 languages, including English, Spanish, German, and Chinese. 
Is there a limit to how many bills I can process?
There’s no limit to how many bills you can scan and import using ApprovalMax Capture. Simply select the right subscription level for your volume. If you reach your limit, we’ll let you know and offer the option of upgrading.
Does ApprovalMax work with documents captured in Dext?
Yes. By connecting ApprovalMax with Dext, you can import supplier invoices and bills directly into ApprovalMax for approval before they go into your accounting system.
