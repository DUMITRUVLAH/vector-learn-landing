FAQ
Which plan are credit note workflows available on?
Credit note approval workflows are included from the Standard plan upwards.
Does this work for both sales and purchase credit notes?
Yes. ApprovalMax supports dedicated workflows for both Accounts Payable credit notes (credits you receive from suppliers) and Accounts Receivable credit notes (refunds you issue to customers).
Can I use OCR to upload credit notes?

Yes. Email credit notes directly into ApprovalMax or upload them via the mobile or web app, and ApprovalMax Capture extracts the key details. Capture currently supports AP credit notes only.

Do approvers need access to Xero?
No. Approvers review and approve credit notes in ApprovalMax, by email, or in the mobile app. Your Xero organisation stays untouched by people who only need to sign things off.
Can I create a credit note directly in ApprovalMax?
Yes. Credit notes can be created and submitted for approval directly in ApprovalMax, without switching to your accounting software first.
What happens after a credit note is approved?
It syncs to Xero as Authorised with its full approval history attached, ready for reconciliation.
How does credit note approval work in ApprovalMax?
Supplier (AP) and customer (AR) credit notes each run through a configurable, conditional multi-step approval workflow, then sync to Xero as Authorised with a full history and audit trail.
