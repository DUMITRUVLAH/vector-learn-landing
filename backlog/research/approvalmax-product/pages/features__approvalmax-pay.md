FAQ
Who can use ApprovalMax Pay?

Companies with a UK entity or UK bank account can use ApprovalMax Pay. We are extending availability to additional regions and platforms over time.

Businesses can use ApprovalMax Pay in two ways:

1. Via Wallets, by funding a dedicated payment balance inside ApprovalMax Pay

2. Via Open Banking, using a connected UK bank account

How is ApprovalMax Pay priced?

ApprovalMax Pay uses different pricing models depending on the payment method you choose.

There are no hidden fees, and charges are clearly shown before you make a payment.

1. Wallets works on a pay-as-you-go pricing model. You can find information on how fees are charged here.

2. Open Banking payments are included as part of your subscription (as of March 2026).

Can I use ApprovalMax Pay during the trial?
Yes. ApprovalMax Pay is included in the 14-day trial for Xero organisations in the UK.
Which plans include ApprovalMax Pay?
ApprovalMax Pay is available in all plans for UK businesses using Xero, including Standard, Advanced, and Premium.
What currencies can I pay in?
Wallets support payments in 30+ currencies, including international SWIFT payments and currency conversion. Open Banking payments are currently available in GBP.
Is there a limit on how many bills I can pay at once?
A batch payment can contain up to 200 bills, across multiple currencies and suppliers, sent as a single run. Running payroll, paying contractors or settling supplier invoices, including complex approvals, is easy. 
Do approvers need access to my accounting system?
No. Approvers act in ApprovalMax and don't need a Xero licence to review or approve a payment.
Do approval workflows apply to both Open Banking and Wallet payments?
Yes. All payments, regardless of method, must pass through ApprovalMax's approval workflows, role-based permissions, supplier controls, and audit trail. The payment method does not bypass your controls.
How secure is ApprovalMax Pay?

Security is central to ApprovalMax Pay:

1. Open Banking uses secure authentication and encrypted data sharing

2. Wallets are protected by a regulated payment infrastructure and internal controls

3. ApprovalMax holds security certifications, including ISO 27001

All payments follow ApprovalMax's approval workflows and audit trails.
Is ApprovalMax Pay a bank?
No. ApprovalMax Pay is not a bank and does not provide banking services. Wallet balances are used solely for payment execution purposes and are provided through a regulated payment infrastructure. ApprovalMax Pay enables payment initiation and management, but does not offer deposit-taking services.
