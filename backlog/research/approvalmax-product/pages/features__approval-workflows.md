FAQ
Which plans include multi-level approval workflow?
All of them. Multi-level approval is how every workflow built in ApprovalMax functions - not a separate feature
Is there a limit on how many approval steps or approvers I can have?
There is no limitation on the number of approval steps in a workflow. The plan size you choose covers monthly volume, including the number of documents approved and the number of approvers.
Which accounting platforms does multi-level approval work with?
Multi-level approval workflow is available to businesses, accountants and bookkeepers using Xero, QuickBooks Online, and NetSuite. The same approval engine runs across all of them.
Do approvers need access to our accounting system?

No. Approvers only work from email, our web app and our mobile app, so including them costs no extra seats in your accounting system.

Can I set up approval workflows in the free trial?
Yes. Multi-level approval workflow feature is included in your 14-day free trial; no credit card needed.
Can we build workflows for different entities?

Yes. Each entity (known as an Organisation in ApprovalMax) has its own workflows, all running on the same engine, configured per entity. 

What happens if an approver doesn't respond?
Automatic reminders keep pending requests in front of approvers, so nothing relies on someone remembering. If an approver is away, their queue passes to a substitute designated by your administrator, and the workflow keeps moving.
 
How is this different from the approvals built into my accounting platform?
ApprovalMax is built specifically for approval control: multi-level sign-off with as many steps as your policy requires, conditional routing by your rules, segregation of duties, and a full audit trail on every decision - the same engine across Xero, QuickBooks Online, and Oracle NetSuite. Approved documents land in your accounting platform as normal; ApprovalMax handles everything that needs to happen before they do.
