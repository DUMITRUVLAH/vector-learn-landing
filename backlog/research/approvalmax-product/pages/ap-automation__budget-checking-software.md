How ApprovalMax budget control software works
Spend attached to the right budget

Each line is matched to its budget by account code, date and the categories you use in your ledger. A request touching three budgets shows all three.

Know what's left, not just what's spent

Spend is deducted from the budget once a document is approved, or while it's still in approval, so the remaining figure reflects what's committed.

Checks that follow your budget cycle

Monthly, quarterly, annually or year to date, on net or gross amounts. The check runs over the period the budget was set for.

Proof of how the budget was spent

Every approval is recorded and timestamped, so spend against a budget can be accounted for decision by decision.

FAQ
Which plans include budget checking software?

Budget checking is included in the Advanced and Premium plans for businesses using Xero and QuickBooks Online. This feature is also available to NetSuite customers.

Can I try budget checking feature during trial?
Yes. Budget checking is available to try during your 14-day free trial. Just integrate your accounting software and sync your budgets from there.
Which accounting platforms does budget checking work with?

Budget checking is available for organisations running on Xero, QuickBooks Online and NetSuite.

Where can I upload the budgets to ApprovalMax?
Budgets are synced directly from Xero and QuickBooks Online to ApprovalMax. For Xero, you can also upload a CSV file, as long as it uses your Xero account names.
Which documents get checked against the budget?
Bills and Purchase Orders are checked against your budget before approvals.
What happens to a bill that is over budget?

ApprovalMax shows the approver the remaining budget and flags where it would land if the request is approved. The decision stays with the approver.

How does ApprovalMax handle multi-currency budgets?
Amounts in non-default currencies are converted to the organisation's base currency before they're evaluated against the budget.
Do approvers need access to my accounting system?
No. Approvers work in ApprovalMax and never need a licence for your accounting platform.
Can I set an approval step that triggers when a request is over budget?
No. Budget status can't be used as a condition in a workflow or in the approval matrix. Approvers see how a request affects the budget and decide from there.
